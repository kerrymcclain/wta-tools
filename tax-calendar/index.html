Every statutory property tax deadline for the year you pick. When a deadline lands on a weekend or legal holiday, the calendar shows the next business day, as Tax Code § 1.06 allows. Click a section number to read it in the Tax Code Search.

    Source. Deadlines and section citations follow the Texas Comptroller’s Property Tax Law Deadlines, descriptions shortened. Dates marked “or as soon as practicable” and dates that move with an event (for example, 30 days after a notice) are explained under the entry. Laws change; confirm with the Tax Code, your appraisal district or your tax office before relying on a date.

    Weekends and holidays. Under § 1.06, an act due on a Saturday, Sunday or legal state or national holiday is timely on the next regular business day. This calendar counts federal holidays and Texas’s fixed state holidays. It does not count optional holidays or local office closures (§ 1.06(b)); check with your local office.
